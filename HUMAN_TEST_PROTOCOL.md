# Human Test Protocol — 5 users × 2 weeks (Gap E)

The 105 automated tests and every AI gate validate the CODE. Nothing yet validates that the loop
changes a human's behavior. This protocol is the only review that still matters. The recruiting and
running is the owner's work; this document makes it executable in an evening.

## Who to recruit (5 people, deliberately mixed)
1. **Two from the target niche** — discipline/Stoic/Jocko-adjacent, trains regularly, self-serious.
2. **Two ordinary "I want to be better"** — no philosophy interest, busy life, has abandoned apps.
3. **One open skeptic** — someone who finds discipline apps cringe. Their exit interview is gold.

Ask each for a 2-week honest trial on their own phone (install from
https://csvitzer.github.io/SpartanXPrototype/ — Add to Home Screen). No coaching, no reminders from
you, no check-ins before day 7. You are testing the app, not their loyalty.

## What to measure (per user, from their own report + their export file if they'll share it)
- **Day-9 survival** (the cold-user gate's predicted quit point): still using it on day 9? If not —
  which day did they stop, and on which screen?
- **Quick vs full ratio**: how many quick closes vs full reflections? (>90% quick = the reflection
  cadence needs tuning; 0% full = the standard never moves and they should have noticed.)
- **Ceremony friction**: did they ever skip a practice because the app felt like admin? (The #1
  unanimous gate finding — this is the question that validates or kills the quick-close fix.)
- **The voice**: what words do THEY use for the app unprompted? (Listen for "serious/honest" vs
  "military/LARP/cringe" — this decides the voice question with data instead of taste.)
- **Any real behavior change**: one concrete thing they did that they otherwise wouldn't have.
- **Trust**: did they understand their data never leaves the device? Did it matter to them?

## Day-14 exit questions (ask all six, verbatim)
1. Walk me through the last time you opened it. What did you do, tap by tap?
2. What was the most annoying single moment in two weeks?
3. If this app disappeared tomorrow, would anything in your week change?
4. What would you tell a friend this app IS? (their category words, not yours)
5. Did you ever lie to it or fudge a close? What made that easy or hard?
6. Would you pay for anything in it? What, specifically, and how much?

## Success / failure bars (decide BEFORE running — no moving goalposts)
- **Pass:** ≥3/5 alive at day 9; ≥2/5 name a concrete behavior change; the skeptic's exit words are
  not "cringe".
- **Tune:** 2/5 at day 9 → the ceremony fixes were insufficient; revisit cadence before anything else.
- **Fail honestly:** ≤1/5 at day 9 → the audience paradox is real and no mechanic fixes it; the app
  is a personal tool, full stop, and that's the truth the test bought.

## Logistics
- Their data is theirs: if they share an export (System → Backup & Restore → download), the
  quick/full ratio, active days, and predictions are all in the JSON. Never ask for reflections text.
- Take notes verbatim. Users' exact words matter more than your summary of them.
